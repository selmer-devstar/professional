About Me

A product leader focused on human-centered design and outcomes-based leadership. I believe great products are built by deeply understanding the people who use them — and that the most effective teams are aligned around clear, measurable outcomes rather than a backlog of features.

With over 15 years working in SaaS, I have a track record of scaling B2B platforms from growth to maturity, and developing PM teams. I have experience embedding AI into product experiences, including AI agents and automation-led efficiency gains, and I am driven by shipping products that drive real business value.
